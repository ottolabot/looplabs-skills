# Voice-pack format

A voice pack separates reusable style evidence from the generic `write-good-copy` workflow. Personal packs should remain private; project or brand packs may live in the relevant project repository.

```text
<profile>/
├── VOICE.md
└── examples/
    ├── good/
    └── avoid/
```

## `VOICE.md`

Keep this evidence-based and short:

```markdown
# Voice

## Audience

Who this voice usually addresses and the relationship to them.

## Signals to preserve

- vocabulary and recurring phrases;
- sentence length, cadence, fragments, and parentheticals;
- bluntness, humor, uncertainty, or formality;
- preferred openings, endings, and calls to action.

## Avoid

- tones or structures that do not sound like this voice;
- unsupported claims or forms of hype the writer rejects.

## Boundaries

Private facts, topics, or identifying details that must never be reused.
```

## Examples

Put representative finished pieces in `examples/good/`. Put counterexamples with a short note explaining the mismatch in `examples/avoid/`. Prefer a few strong, varied examples over a large archive.

Examples are style evidence only. Never treat claims, links, dates, or anecdotes inside them as current facts for a new draft.
